# Cyber Fraud and IT Act 2000 — Complete Guide

## Governing Laws:
- Information Technology Act, 2000 (IT Act)
- Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS) — replaced IPC
- Reserve Bank of India Circular on Customer Protection (July 2017)
- Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021

---

## Key Sections of IT Act 2000

### Section 43 — Unauthorized Access / Hacking:
If someone hacks into your computer, email, or server without permission.
Penalty: Compensation up to Rs 1 Crore (civil liability)
File complaint: Cyber Crime Portal https://cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930

### Section 66 — Computer Related Offences:
Criminal offense for dishonest or fraudulent computer access.
Penalty: Imprisonment up to 3 years AND/OR fine up to Rs 5 Lakh

### Section 66C — Identity Theft:
Using someone else's digital identity (password, Aadhaar, PAN, digital signature) fraudulently.
Penalty: Imprisonment up to 3 years AND fine up to Rs 1 Lakh
Examples: Using someone's OTP to access their bank, fake Aadhaar, stolen credentials

### Section 66D — Cheating by Personation (Impersonation):
Creating a fake website, app, or social media profile to cheat someone.
Penalty: Imprisonment up to 3 years AND fine up to Rs 1 Lakh
Examples: Fake UPI IDs, phishing websites, fake customer care numbers

### Section 67 — Publishing Obscene Material:
Publishing or transmitting obscene content in electronic form.
Penalty: Up to 5 years imprisonment and Rs 10 Lakh fine

### Section 72 — Breach of Confidentiality and Privacy:
If someone who has access to your personal data (ISP, company) discloses it without consent.
Penalty: Up to 2 years imprisonment and Rs 1 Lakh fine

## Bank Account Fraud — Zero Liability Rule (RBI)

### RBI Circular on Customer Protection (July 6, 2017):
This is the most important rule for banking fraud victims.

| Time of Reporting to Bank | Your Liability |
|---|---|
| Within 3 working days | ZERO — Bank bears full loss |
| 4-7 working days | Limited to Rs 5,000 to Rs 10,000 |
| After 7 working days | Case-by-case, but bank must have grievance process |

### IMPORTANT: What qualifies as Zero Liability:
The fraud must be due to the bank's negligence or a third-party breach (not your own sharing of OTP/PIN).

### What to do if your account is hacked:
1. Call the bank IMMEDIATELY (24x7 fraud helpline)
2. Block all cards
3. Email the bank with "FRAUD COMPLAINT" as subject
4. File FIR at local police station (mandatory for insurance claims)
5. File complaint at: https://cms.rbi.org.in (Banking Ombudsman)
6. Raise complaint with Cyber Crime: https://cybercrime.gov.in

## Digital Evidence — Screenshots Are Admissible

### Section 65B, Indian Evidence Act (Now Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam 2023):
Electronic records (screenshots, WhatsApp messages, emails, transaction histories) are admissible as evidence in Indian courts.

### What you need for screenshots to be valid evidence:
1. A certificate under Section 65B from the device owner or IT department
2. The device from which the screenshot was taken (for authentication if challenged)
3. Metadata preserved (timestamp, device info)

### Practical tip:
- Screenshot WhatsApp chats showing fraud
- Screenshot bank statements and transaction records
- Record all correspondence by email (better than WhatsApp for court purposes)
- Download bank statements as PDF (official copies preferred)

## Types of Cyber Fraud and How to Act

### 1. UPI Fraud / QR Code Scam:
Scammer sends a "receive money" QR code that actually charges you.
Action: Call bank immediately (report within 3 days for zero liability). File at: https://cybercrime.gov.in

### 2. OTP Fraud:
Someone calls pretending to be a bank/government official and asks for OTP.
Rule: NO bank, government body, or company EVER asks for OTP on call.
Action: Block the number, call your bank, report at 1930.

### 3. Fake Job Offer Scam:
Promise of job in exchange for "registration fee" or "security deposit."
Legal route: IT Act 66D (cheating by personation) + BNS Section 318 (cheating).
File FIR and complaint at: https://cybercrime.gov.in

### 4. Investment/Ponzi Scam:
Promises of guaranteed 30-50% returns.
Legal route: SEBI complaint for investment fraud https://scores.sebi.gov.in
FIR under BNS Section 316 (criminal breach of trust)

### 5. Social Media Hacking / Morphed Images:
Someone hacks your account or creates fake profile.
IT Act Section 66C and 66E (violation of privacy).
File: https://cybercrime.gov.in → Report Cybercrime → Other Cyber Crime

## Key Case Laws

1. **Shreya Singhal vs Union of India (2015):** Section 66A (online speech) struck down, but other sections remain valid
2. **Avnish Bajaj vs State (2005):** Platform liability for third-party content on e-commerce sites
3. **Christian Louboutin SAS vs Nakul Bajaj (2018):** E-commerce platforms liable for counterfeits if they have actual knowledge

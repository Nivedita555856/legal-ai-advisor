# Information Technology Act, 2000 — Cyber Offences and Data Protection

**Statute:** Information Technology Act, 2000 (Act No. 21 of 2000)
**Date of Enactment:** 17 October 2000
**Major Amendment:** Information Technology (Amendment) Act, 2008

## Section 43: Penalty and Compensation for Damage to Computer System

If any person without permission of the owner or any other person in charge of a computer, computer system, or computer network:
- Accesses or secures access to such computer
- Downloads, copies, or extracts any data
- Introduces or causes to be introduced any computer contaminant or virus
- Damages or causes damage to any computer or data residing therein
- Disrupts or causes disruption of any computer
- Denies access to any authorized user
- Charges services availed by a person to another person by manipulating the computer
- Destroys, deletes, or alters information residing in a computer
- Steals, conceals, destroys, or alters any computer source code

...shall be liable to pay damages by way of compensation to the person so affected, which may extend up to Rs. 5 crore for unauthorized access and data breaches.

## Section 43A: Compensation for Failure to Protect Data

Where a body corporate, possessing, dealing, or handling any sensitive personal data or information in a computer resource which it owns, controls, or operates, is negligent in implementing and maintaining reasonable security practices and procedures, and thereby causes wrongful loss or wrongful gain to any person, such body corporate shall be liable to pay damages by way of compensation, not exceeding Rs. 5 crore, to the person so affected.

**Note:** "Sensitive personal data" includes passwords, financial information (bank account, credit card, debit card details), physical, physiological, and mental health condition, sexual orientation, medical records, and biometric information.

## Section 65: Tampering with Computer Source Documents

Whoever knowingly or intentionally conceals, destroys, or alters or intentionally or knowingly causes another to conceal, destroy, or alter any computer source code used for a computer, computer programme, computer system, or computer network, when the computer source code is required to be kept or maintained by law, shall be punishable with:
- Imprisonment up to three (3) years, OR
- Fine up to Rs. 2 lakh, OR
- Both

## Section 66: Computer Related Offences

If any person, dishonestly or fraudulently, does any act referred to in Section 43, shall be punishable with:
- Imprisonment up to three (3) years
- Fine up to Rs. 5 lakhs
- Or both

## Section 66B: Punishment for Dishonestly Receiving Stolen Computer Resource

Whoever dishonestly receives or retains any stolen computer resource or communication device, knowing or having reason to believe the same to be stolen, shall be punished with imprisonment up to three (3) years or fine up to Rs. 1 lakh or both.

## Section 66C: Punishment for Identity Theft

Whoever fraudulently or dishonestly makes use of the electronic signature, password, or any other unique identification feature of any other person, shall be punished with:
- Imprisonment up to three (3) years
- Fine up to Rs. 1 lakh

## Section 66D: Cheating by Personation Using Computer Resource

Whoever, by means of any communication device or computer resource cheats by personation, shall be punished with:
- Imprisonment up to three (3) years
- Fine up to Rs. 1 lakh

This section covers phishing, fake websites, fraudulent email communications, and impersonation on social media for financial fraud.

## Section 66E: Violation of Privacy

Whoever, intentionally or knowingly captures, publishes, or transmits the image of a private area of any person without his or her consent, under circumstances violating the privacy of that person, shall be punished with:
- Imprisonment up to three (3) years
- Fine up to Rs. 2 lakhs
- Or both

## Section 66F: Cyber Terrorism

(1) Whoever, with intent to threaten the unity, integrity, security, or sovereignty of India or to strike terror, denies access to computer resources, attempts to penetrate restricted access computers, or introduces any contaminant likely to cause death, injuries, damage to property, or disruption of essential services...

shall be punishable with imprisonment which may extend to imprisonment for life.

## Section 67: Publishing Obscene Material in Electronic Form

Whoever publishes or transmits or causes to be published or transmitted in electronic form any material which is lascivious or appeals to prurient interest, or if its effect is such as to tend to deprave and corrupt persons...

- First conviction: imprisonment up to three (3) years and fine up to Rs. 5 lakhs
- Subsequent conviction: imprisonment up to five (5) years and fine up to Rs. 10 lakhs

## Section 67A: Sexually Explicit Material

Publishing or transmitting sexually explicit material in electronic form:
- First conviction: imprisonment up to five (5) years and fine up to Rs. 10 lakhs
- Subsequent conviction: imprisonment up to seven (7) years and fine up to Rs. 10 lakhs

## Section 67B: Child Pornography and Sexual Abuse Material

Publishing, transmitting, or causing to be published material depicting children in sexually explicit acts in electronic form, or facilitating online abuse of children:
- First conviction: imprisonment up to five (5) years and fine up to Rs. 10 lakhs
- Subsequent conviction: imprisonment up to seven (7) years and fine up to Rs. 10 lakhs

## Section 69: Power to Intercept, Monitor, or Decrypt

The Central Government or State Government, when satisfied that it is necessary in the interest of sovereignty, integrity, defence, security, public order, friendly relations with foreign states, or for preventing incitement to commission of any cognizable offence, may direct any agency of the appropriate government to intercept, monitor, or decrypt any information.

The subscriber or intermediary who fails to comply with such direction shall be punished with imprisonment up to seven (7) years and fine.

## Section 70: Protected Systems

The appropriate Government may declare any computer resource which directly or indirectly affects the facility of Critical Information Infrastructure to be a "protected system". Unauthorized access to such a protected system shall be punishable with imprisonment up to ten (10) years and fine.

## Section 72: Penalty for Breach of Confidentiality and Privacy

Any person who, in pursuance of any of the powers conferred under the Act, has secured access to any electronic record, book, register, correspondence, information, document, or other material, and discloses such information without the consent of the person concerned, shall be punished with imprisonment up to two (2) years or fine up to Rs. 1 lakh or both.

## Section 72A: Disclosure of Information in Breach of Lawful Contract

Any person, including an intermediary, who, while providing services under terms of a lawful contract, discloses personal information without consent or in breach of contract, with intent to cause wrongful loss or gain, shall be punished with imprisonment up to three (3) years or fine up to Rs. 5 lakhs or both.

## Section 79: Intermediary Liability ("Safe Harbour")

(1) Notwithstanding anything contained in any law for the time being in force, an intermediary shall not be liable for any third party information, data, or communication link made available or hosted by it.

(2) The protection applies if:
- The intermediary's function is limited to providing access to a communication system
- It does not initiate the transmission, select the receiver, or modify the information
- It observes due diligence and the IT (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules, 2021

(3) The intermediary loses safe harbour if it fails to remove unlawful content upon receiving "actual knowledge" by way of a court order or notification from the appropriate government.

## Reporting Cyber Crimes

Cyber crimes may be reported through:
- **National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal:** https://cybercrime.gov.in
- **Cyber Helpline:** 1930 (financial fraud)
- Local cyber crime police stations
- Filing FIR at any police station (cybercrime is bailable except for serious offences)

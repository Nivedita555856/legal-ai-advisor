# Residential Rental Agreement Template (Karnataka)

**Document Type:** Lease Deed
**Jurisdiction:** State of Karnataka, India
**Governing Act:** Karnataka Rent Act, 1999 and Transfer of Property Act, 1882

## 1. Parties

This Rental Agreement ("Agreement") is executed on [DATE] between:

**LANDLORD:** [Name], aged [Age] years, residing at [Address], hereinafter referred to as the "Lessor".

**TENANT:** [Name], aged [Age] years, residing at [Address], hereinafter referred to as the "Lessee".

## 2. Premises

The Lessor agrees to let out the residential property located at [Full Address], comprising of [Number] BHK with carpet area of approximately [X] sq. ft., to the Lessee for residential use only.

## 3. Term and Lock-in Period

3.1 The term of this lease shall be eleven (11) months commencing from [Start Date] and ending on [End Date].

3.2 There shall be a lock-in period of six (6) months from the commencement date. Neither party may terminate this agreement before the expiry of the lock-in period, except for material breach by the other party.

3.3 The Agreement may be renewed for further periods of eleven months by mutual written consent, with rent revision not exceeding 7% per annum.

## 4. Rent

4.1 The monthly rent shall be Rs. [Amount] (Rupees [Amount in words]) payable in advance on or before the 5th day of each English calendar month.

4.2 Late payment beyond 7 days of due date shall attract a late fee of Rs. 500 per occurrence.

4.3 Rent shall be paid through bank transfer (NEFT/UPI) to the Lessor's designated account. Cash payments shall not be valid unless acknowledged through a signed receipt.

## 5. Security Deposit

5.1 The Lessee has paid a refundable interest-free security deposit of Rs. [Amount] equivalent to ten (10) months' rent to the Lessor on execution of this Agreement.

5.2 The security deposit shall be refunded to the Lessee within thirty (30) days of vacating the premises, after deducting:
- Any unpaid rent or utility bills
- Cost of repairs for damages beyond normal wear and tear
- Any other dues under this Agreement

5.3 The Lessor shall not deduct from the security deposit for painting, deep cleaning, or normal wear and tear in the absence of damage.

## 6. Termination and Notice Period

6.1 After the lock-in period of six months, either party may terminate this Agreement by giving sixty (60) days prior written notice to the other party.

6.2 If the Lessee vacates before completing the lock-in period without material breach by the Lessor, the Lessee shall forfeit two (2) months' rent from the security deposit as compensation.

6.3 The Lessor may terminate this Agreement with thirty (30) days notice in the event of:
- Non-payment of rent for two consecutive months
- Material damage to the premises
- Use of premises for unlawful purposes
- Sub-letting without written consent

6.4 The Lessor shall not evict the Lessee without due legal process under the Karnataka Rent Act.

## 7. Maintenance and Repairs

7.1 The Lessor shall be responsible for major structural repairs including roof, walls, plumbing main lines, and electrical wiring.

7.2 The Lessee shall be responsible for minor repairs not exceeding Rs. 2,000 per occurrence, including bulbs, taps, and minor fittings.

7.3 The Lessee shall pay all consumption-based utility bills (electricity, water, gas) directly to the service providers.

7.4 Maintenance charges levied by the apartment association shall be borne by the Lessor.

## 8. Use of Premises

8.1 The premises shall be used exclusively for residential purposes.

8.2 The Lessee shall not carry out any commercial activity, store hazardous materials, or cause nuisance to neighbours.

8.3 Pets are permitted with prior written consent of the Lessor, subject to apartment association rules.

8.4 The Lessee shall not make structural alterations without prior written consent of the Lessor.

## 9. Sub-letting and Assignment

The Lessee shall not assign, sub-let, or part with possession of the premises or any portion thereof to any third party without prior written consent of the Lessor. Violation of this clause shall be grounds for immediate termination.

## 10. Inspection

The Lessor or its authorized representative may inspect the premises with 48 hours prior notice to the Lessee, at a reasonable time, not more than once per quarter.

## 11. Force Majeure

Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform obligations arising due to force majeure events including natural disasters, government action, pandemic, or other circumstances beyond reasonable control.

## 12. Dispute Resolution

12.1 Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall first be attempted to be resolved through mutual discussion.

12.2 If unresolved within 30 days, the dispute shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, with a sole arbitrator mutually appointed. The seat of arbitration shall be Bengaluru, Karnataka.

12.3 Courts in Bengaluru shall have exclusive jurisdiction subject to the arbitration clause above.

## 13. Stamp Duty and Registration

13.1 This Agreement shall be executed on appropriate stamp paper as per Karnataka Stamp Act.

13.2 For lease periods of 12 months or more, the Agreement shall be registered with the sub-registrar.

13.3 Registration costs shall be shared equally between Lessor and Lessee unless otherwise agreed.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first written above in the presence of witnesses.

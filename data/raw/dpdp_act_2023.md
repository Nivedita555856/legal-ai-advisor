# Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)

## Governing Law: DPDP Act, 2023 + DPDP Rules, 2025

Passed by Parliament: August 11, 2023. DPDP Rules notified in 2025.
India's first comprehensive data protection law.

---

## Key Definitions

### Data Principal (Section 2(j)):
The individual to whom the personal data relates. That is YOU — the user whose data is collected.

### Data Fiduciary (Section 2(i)):
Any person (company, app, website, government body) that determines the purpose and means of processing personal data. Examples: Zomato, Netflix, your employer, a hospital, a bank.

### Personal Data (Section 2(t)):
Any data about an identifiable individual. Includes name, phone number, Aadhaar, location, browsing history, biometric data, financial data.

---

## Section 6 — Consent Requirements

A Data Fiduciary MUST:
1. Obtain FREE, SPECIFIC, INFORMED and UNCONDITIONAL consent before processing your data
2. Provide notice in clear, plain language (not buried in 50-page terms)
3. NOT bundle consent (you cannot be forced to accept all or nothing)
4. Allow you to withdraw consent at any time

What companies CANNOT do:
- Force you to consent to irrelevant data collection to use the service
- Deny service simply because you refuse to share location data for a banking app
- Use confusing language to get blanket consent

---

## Section 8 — Rights of Data Principal (YOUR Rights)

### Right to Information:
You can ask any company "What data do you have about me?" They must respond within reasonable time.

### Right to Correction and Erasure (Right to be Forgotten):
You have the legal right to demand a company (Zomato, Netflix, your old employer, a hospital) to DELETE ALL YOUR PERSONAL DATA permanently.

How to exercise: Send a formal written request to the company's Data Protection Officer (DPO). If they refuse or ignore, file a complaint with the Data Protection Board.

### Right to Grievance Redressal:
Every Data Fiduciary must have a grievance officer. You can file a complaint with them. If unsatisfied, escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.

### Right to Nominate:
You can nominate someone to exercise your data rights on your behalf in case of death or incapacity.

---

## Significant Data Fiduciaries

Certain large companies with massive data (likely Meta, Google, major banks) will be designated as "Significant Data Fiduciaries" with additional obligations including:
- Data Protection Officer must be based in India
- Data Audits
- Data Protection Impact Assessments

---

## Data Breach — 72-Hour Notification Rule

If a company suffers a data breach involving your personal data:
- They MUST notify the Data Protection Board of India within 72 hours
- They MUST notify you (the affected individual) promptly
- Failure to notify: Fine up to Rs 200 Crore

If your Aadhaar number, financial data, health records are leaked:
- File complaint with Data Protection Board
- File complaint with UIDAI if Aadhaar is involved: https://uidai.gov.in
- File FIR under IT Act Section 66C (Identity Theft)

---

## Consent Managers

Under DPDP Rules 2025, Consent Manager platforms (like apps) will be created where you can:
- See all companies that have your consent
- Withdraw consent from multiple companies at once
- Manage data permissions centrally

This is similar to having a "master switch" for all your data consents.

---

## Penalties Under DPDP Act

| Violation | Maximum Fine |
|---|---|
| Breach of data of children | Rs 200 Crore |
| Failure to take security safeguards (causing data breach) | Rs 250 Crore |
| Non-compliance with Board directives | Rs 150 Crore |
| Breach of Significant Data Fiduciary obligations | Rs 150 Crore |
| Non-fulfilment of obligations to Data Principal | Rs 50 Crore |

---

## Special Protections for Children's Data

- Verifiable parental consent required before processing data of anyone under 18
- Companies CANNOT show targeted advertising to children
- Companies CANNOT profile children
- Social media platforms must verify age before allowing accounts

---

## What to Do If Your Data is Misused

1. Contact the company's Data Protection Officer (email or portal)
2. If no response in 30 days: File complaint with Data Protection Board of India
3. For Aadhaar misuse: https://uidai.gov.in → File complaint
4. For banking data: RBI Ombudsman → https://cms.rbi.org.in
5. For cyber crime involving data: https://cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930

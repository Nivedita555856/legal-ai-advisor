# Employment Contract Template (Full-Time, IT Sector)

**Document Type:** Letter of Appointment / Employment Agreement
**Jurisdiction:** India (Industrial Employment Standing Orders Act; Shops and Establishments Act of the relevant state)

## 1. Parties

This Employment Agreement is between:

**EMPLOYER:** [Company Name] Pvt. Ltd., a private limited company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at [Address] (the "Company").

**EMPLOYEE:** [Name], aged [Age] years, residing at [Address] (the "Employee").

## 2. Position and Duties

2.1 The Company hereby appoints the Employee as [Designation] reporting to [Reporting Manager Title].

2.2 The Employee shall perform duties as outlined in the Job Description attached as Annexure A and such other duties as may be reasonably assigned from time to time.

2.3 The Employee shall devote their full working time and attention to the Company's business and shall not engage in any other employment, business, or activity, paid or unpaid, without prior written consent of the Company.

## 3. Commencement and Probation

3.1 Employment shall commence on [Joining Date].

3.2 The Employee shall serve a probationary period of six (6) months from the date of joining. During probation, employment may be terminated by either party with seven (7) days' written notice or pay in lieu thereof.

3.3 Upon successful completion of probation, the Employee shall be confirmed as a permanent employee in writing.

## 4. Compensation

4.1 The Employee's total Cost-to-Company (CTC) shall be Rs. [Amount] per annum, broken down as per Annexure B (Salary Structure).

4.2 The salary shall be paid monthly by the 7th of the following month through direct bank transfer.

4.3 The Company shall deduct applicable taxes including TDS, Provident Fund, and Professional Tax as per law.

4.4 The Employee shall be eligible for an annual performance bonus subject to individual and Company performance, at the sole discretion of the Company.

## 5. Working Hours and Leave

5.1 Standard working hours are 9 hours per day, 5 days per week (Monday to Friday), with one hour lunch break.

5.2 The Employee shall be entitled to:
- 12 days of casual leave per calendar year
- 12 days of sick leave per calendar year
- 18 days of earned leave per calendar year (encashable up to 30 days at separation)
- 10 paid public holidays as notified by the Company
- 26 weeks of maternity leave (for eligible female employees as per Maternity Benefit Act)
- 15 days of paternity leave for new fathers

5.3 Unused casual and sick leave shall lapse at the end of the calendar year and shall not be encashed.

## 6. Notice Period and Termination

6.1 After confirmation, either party may terminate this employment by giving the following notice in writing:
- Employee resigning: ninety (90) days written notice
- Company terminating without cause: ninety (90) days written notice or pay in lieu

6.2 The Company may terminate employment without notice or pay in lieu for cause, including but not limited to:
- Wilful misconduct or insubordination
- Theft, fraud, or dishonesty
- Breach of confidentiality or non-compete obligations
- Conviction of a criminal offence involving moral turpitude
- Persistent poor performance after warnings
- Unauthorized absence exceeding seven consecutive days

6.3 During the notice period, the Company may at its discretion:
- Place the Employee on garden leave
- Restrict access to confidential information or premises
- Require knowledge transfer to successors

6.4 The Employee shall not be entitled to gratuity if they have rendered less than five (5) years of continuous service, except in cases of death or disablement.

## 7. Confidentiality and Intellectual Property

7.1 During and after employment, the Employee shall maintain strict confidentiality regarding all Company information including business plans, customer data, financial information, source code, and trade secrets.

7.2 All intellectual property created by the Employee in the course of employment, including but not limited to inventions, code, designs, and content, shall be the exclusive property of the Company.

7.3 The Employee assigns all rights, title, and interest in such intellectual property to the Company and shall execute any documents necessary to perfect such rights.

## 8. Non-Compete and Non-Solicitation

8.1 During employment and for a period of twelve (12) months thereafter, the Employee shall not directly or indirectly:
- Engage in any business that competes with the Company in India
- Solicit or attempt to hire any Company employee
- Solicit Company customers, vendors, or partners for competing business

8.2 The geographic scope and duration of non-compete shall be enforceable only to the extent permitted by Indian law (subject to Section 27 of the Indian Contract Act).

## 9. Code of Conduct

9.1 The Employee shall comply with the Company's HR policies, Code of Conduct, Anti-Sexual Harassment Policy (POSH), and IT Acceptable Use Policy, all of which are accessible on the Company's HR portal.

9.2 The Employee shall comply with all applicable laws and refrain from any conduct that may bring the Company into disrepute.

## 10. Data Protection

10.1 The Employee consents to the Company collecting, processing, and storing personal data for employment purposes in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

10.2 Employee data shall be retained for a period of seven (7) years after separation for legal and statutory purposes.

## 11. Resolution of Disputes

11.1 Any dispute shall first be attempted to be resolved through internal grievance mechanisms.

11.2 Unresolved disputes shall be subject to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, with the seat at [City].

11.3 Industrial disputes shall be governed by the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 where applicable.

## 12. Severability and Entire Agreement

If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force. This Agreement, along with the Annexures and Company policies referenced herein, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.

## 13. Acceptance

By signing below, the Employee acknowledges reading, understanding, and accepting all terms of this Agreement.

[Employer Signature]                          [Employee Signature]
